Delete Your Account

Request permanent deletion of your Lens account and personal data.

This action is permanent. Once your account is deleted, you will lose access to your bookings, messages, saved photographers and any photos delivered through Lens. This cannot be undone.

Option 1: Delete from the app

The fastest way is from inside the Lens app:

  1. Open the Lens app and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile then Settings.
  3. Tap Account then Delete Account.
  4. Confirm with your password or a one-time code.

Option 2: Request deletion here

If you can’t access the app, send us a request using the form below. Use the email address registered with your Lens account so we can verify it’s you.

What happens to your data

Deleted

  • Profile details and profile photo
  • Saved photographers and studios
  • Messages and reviews you wrote
  • Location and device data
  • Portfolio images (photographers and studios)

May be retained

  • Payment and invoice records, as required by tax and accounting laws
  • Records needed to resolve disputes or enforce our terms
  • Fraud-prevention and security logs

Retained data is kept only for as long as legally required and is then deleted or anonymized.

Timeline

StepWhen
Verification of your requestWithin 3 business days
Account deactivatedImmediately after verification
Personal data permanently deletedWithin 30 days
Backups fully purgedWithin 90 days

Before you delete

  • Upcoming bookings: cancel or complete them first. Cancellation and refund rules still apply.
  • Download your photos: save any delivered session photos you want to keep.
  • Photographers and studios: any pending payouts will be settled before deletion.
  • Just taking a break? You can deactivate your account in the app instead and come back later.
