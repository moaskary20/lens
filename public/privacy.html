Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Lens (“Lens”, “we”, “us”, “our”). Lens is a platform that lets you discover, compare and book professional photographers and photography studios. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use and share it, and the choices you have when you use our mobile application and website (together, the “Service”).

By using Lens, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the Service.

2. Information We Collect

Information you provide

  • Account details: name, email address, phone number, password and profile photo.
  • Booking details: selected photographer or studio, date, time, location, session type and any notes you add.
  • Payment information: processed by our third-party payment providers. We do not store full card numbers.
  • Communications: messages with photographers, studios and our support team, plus reviews and ratings.
  • Photographer and studio profiles: business name, portfolio images, pricing, availability and verification documents.

Information collected automatically

  • Device data: device model, operating system, app version, language and unique identifiers.
  • Usage data: pages viewed, searches, features used, and timestamps.
  • Location data: approximate or precise location, only with your permission, to show nearby photographers and studios.
  • Cookies and similar technologies (see Section 7).

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Create and manage your account.
  • Process bookings, payments, refunds and cancellations.
  • Connect you with photographers and studios and enable in-app messaging.
  • Show relevant search results and recommendations.
  • Send booking confirmations, reminders and service notifications.
  • Provide customer support and resolve disputes.
  • Detect and prevent fraud, abuse and security incidents.
  • Improve, test and develop the Service.
  • Comply with legal obligations.

5. Photos and User Content

Photographers and studios may upload portfolio images and deliver session photos through Lens. Photos delivered to you after a session are visible only to you and the photographer unless you choose to share them. Photographers are responsible for obtaining any required consent from people who appear in their portfolio images. Rights in the photos themselves are governed by the agreement between you and the photographer or studio.

6. App Permissions

  • Location: to find nearby photographers and studios. Optional.
  • Camera and photo library: to upload a profile picture or reference images. Optional.
  • Notifications: for booking updates and messages. Optional.
  • Calendar: to add bookings to your calendar. Optional.

You can change these permissions at any time in your device settings.

7. Cookies and Tracking

On our website we use cookies and similar technologies to keep you signed in, remember preferences, measure traffic and improve performance. You can control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling some cookies may affect how the Service works.

8. Data Retention

We keep your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the Service. We may retain certain data after account deletion to comply with legal, tax and accounting obligations, resolve disputes and enforce our agreements. When data is no longer needed, we delete or anonymize it.

9. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures, including encryption in transit and access controls, to protect your information. However, no system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. Please use a strong, unique password and keep it confidential.

10. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Delete your account and personal data.
  • Object to or restrict certain processing.
  • Receive a copy of your data in a portable format.
  • Withdraw consent at any time, where processing is based on consent.
  • Opt out of marketing messages using the unsubscribe link or app settings.

To exercise these rights, contact us using the details in Section 14. We may need to verify your identity before responding.

Account deletion: you can request deletion of your account and data from the app settings or by emailing us.

11. Children’s Privacy

Lens is not intended for children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

12. International Data Transfers

Your information may be processed and stored in countries other than your own, where data protection laws may differ. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards to protect your information when it is transferred.
